What it does

It keeps the chat alive. That's the job.

Your league text is unhinged in September and dead by Halloween. Commish is the member who never gets bored, never misses a week, and never lets a bad start go unmentioned.

The Tuesday reckoning

A weekly recap that names names. It knows what your optimal lineup would have scored, so "unlucky" is not available to you as an excuse. Your commissioner approves it before the league sees it — someone should be holding the leash.

It saves you, then mocks you

Starting someone who's Out, IR or PUP? You'll hear about it before that player's kickoff — including the 9:30am games in Germany that ruin people every year. Ignore the warning and it will absolutely bring it up on Tuesday.

Trades get graded. Just not yet.

Every trade gets announced with no grade attached, because on day one nobody knows anything. Three weeks later it comes back and settles it with points those players actually scored. The trade everyone mocked you for finally gets its day.

Say its name and it answers

Ask who won the league in 2023, who has the worst bench luck, what your record is against Dave. It knows, because it's been reading the whole time. Don't talk to it and it shuts up — silence is its default, not an oversight.

It checks its work.

Every number in a recap is verified against your league's own data before the message is allowed to send, and one that can't back up a claim gets held instead of posted. This matters twice over: an alert you can't trust is worse than no alert, and a bot that talks trash and gets the score wrong is just embarrassing. It's the same reason trades aren't graded on day one — an instant grade means predicting the rest of the season, and it would rather wait three weeks and be right.

Setup

Five minutes, and nobody has to download anything.

  1. Type your Sleeper username. We'll pull up your leagues and you pick one — no digging through app settings for an ID.
  2. Add the number to your group chat. The one you already use. iPhone and Android in the same thread is fine — that was the whole requirement this thing was built around.
  3. Everyone texts their name once. That's how it learns which phone is which roster, so it knows exactly who to blame.

Sleeper only for now. No app, no new group chat, nothing for your league to sign up for.

Messaging terms

What you're agreeing to.

How you opt in

Your league commissioner adds the Commish number to your league's group text. Being in that group chat is the opt-in — messages arrive in the thread you're already part of. Nobody is messaged individually without asking for it first.

How you opt out

Reply STOP at any time and you'll get no further messages. Reply HELP for contact information. You can also simply leave the group chat, or ask your commissioner to remove the number from it.

Your phone number is used to send these messages and to tell managers apart within your league. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing. Details are in the privacy policy.
