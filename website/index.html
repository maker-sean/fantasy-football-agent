What it does

It keeps the chat alive. That's the job.

Your league text is unhinged in September and dead by Halloween. Commish is the member who never gets bored, never misses a week, and never lets a bad start go unmentioned.

The Tuesday Reckoning

Leave points on your bench and it does the math. It knows exactly what your optimal lineup would have scored, so "unlucky" is not available to you as an excuse. What you left there goes on the record too.

The Lineup Bailiff

Start someone who's Out, IR or PUP and it warns you before that player's kickoff, including the 9:30am games in Germany that ruin people every year. Ignore the warning and it brings it up again on Tuesday.

The Transaction Czar

Win a player by a dollar and it points out the dollar. Blow $40 on a player nobody else bid on and it says so, unprompted. Dump half your roster in one week and it will have thoughts about how your draft went.

How it works

Three steps, and only one of them is yours.

Commish reads your Sleeper league and posts into the group text you already use. No app to install, no second chat to keep alive, and nothing for the rest of your league to do. The first week is free.

  1. 1

    Pick your league

    Type your Sleeper username, choose the league, and put in everyone's name and number while you are there. That part takes about a minute.

  2. 2

    Add Commish to the chat

    Add the number to your league's group text. The thread you already use. iPhone and Android together is fine.

  3. 3

    Start getting roasted

    It reads the league from there. Nobody else has to sign up, download anything, or text it back. The first reckoning lands Tuesday.

Sleeper leagues only for now.

Questions

What your league will ask.

We have iPhones and Androids in the same group chat. Does that work?

That is the requirement this was built around, not a feature bolted on afterwards. Commish sends over iMessage, RCS or SMS depending on what each person is carrying, in the one thread your league already uses. Nobody switches apps and nobody starts a second chat.

Does everyone in my league have to sign up?

No, and this is the part people expect to be worse than it is. You put in everyone's name and number once while you are setting it up, and that is the end of anybody else's involvement. No app, no account, no confirmation text to reply to.

How do I change the name on a team?

Names are set once during setup, and editing them afterwards is not self-serve yet. It is the next thing being built. Until then, ask and we will change it.

What happens when we start a new season, or a new league?

You add the new league the same way you added the first one. Sleeper treats each season as its own league, so history does not carry across automatically today. Nothing you have already set up is lost.

What if it gets annoying?

Silence is what it does by default: it stays quiet unless something actually happened or someone addressed it directly. Any individual can reply STOP and hear nothing again, and the commissioner can dial the tone down or remove the number entirely.

Does it work with ESPN or Yahoo?

Not yet. Sleeper only, because Sleeper is the one with an open API that reports what every manager actually started. Guessing at the other two would mean a bot that is confidently wrong about your lineup, which is worse than no bot.
