What it actually does

Four things, all of them true.

Everything here is shipped and running. There is no roadmap on this page pretending to be a feature list.

The Tuesday recap

Written from your league's actual scoring — including the points you left on your bench. Your commissioner approves it by text before anyone else sees it.

Pre-kickoff injury alerts

If you're starting someone who is Out, IR, or PUP, you hear about it before that player's game — not before a guessed-at Sunday noon. International games and Thursday nights included.

Trades, announced and then judged

The trade gets announced when it happens, with no grade attached. Three weeks later it comes back with a grade built from points those players actually scored.

It answers when you ask

Say its name in the chat and it responds using your league's real history. Ignore it and it stays quiet — silence is the default, not an accident.

It doesn't make things up

Every number in a recap is checked against the league's own data before the message is allowed to send. If a claim can't be traced to a real stat, the recap is held back rather than posted. This is why trades aren't graded on the day they happen: an instant grade requires predicting the rest of the season, and there's no honest way to do that.

Setup

Three steps, about five minutes.

  1. Paste your Sleeper league ID. It's in the Sleeper app under league settings.
  2. Add the number to your group chat. The same thread you already use. iPhone and Android together is fine — that was the requirement this was built around.
  3. Everyone texts their name once. That's how the agent learns which phone belongs to which roster, so it can tell you apart.

Sleeper only for now. No app to install, no new group to create, nothing for your league to sign up for.

Messaging terms

What you're agreeing to.

How you opt in

Your league commissioner adds the Commish number to your league's group text. Being in that group chat is the opt-in — messages arrive in the thread you're already part of. Nobody is messaged individually without asking for it first.

How you opt out

Reply STOP at any time and you'll get no further messages. Reply HELP for contact information. You can also simply leave the group chat, or ask your commissioner to remove the number from it.
