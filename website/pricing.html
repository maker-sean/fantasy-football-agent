Questions your league will ask

Does everyone need an iPhone?

No. Mixed iPhone and Android group chats were the requirement this was built around. Three messaging providers were tested and the first two failed on exactly that.

Do we have to make a new group chat?

No. It joins the thread you already use. That's the whole point, because a bot in a group nobody opens is a bot nobody reads.

Does it read everything we say?

It reads the group chat it's in, so it can tell when it's being addressed and answer with context. It replies only when spoken to. What's collected and where it goes is spelled out in the privacy policy, including that message content is sent to an AI provider to generate replies.

Can it post something wrong?

Every figure in a recap is checked against your league's own data before it can send, and one that cannot be backed up is held rather than posted. It jokes about football decisions and nothing else: never someone's job, family, appearance or intelligence.

What if it gets annoying?

Reply STOP, or have your commissioner remove the number. There are also hard limits on how often it will speak, and silence is its default state.

What about ESPN or Yahoo leagues?

Sleeper only right now. We'd rather say that plainly than list a platform we don't really support.
