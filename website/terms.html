What the service is

Commish is an automated agent that participates in your fantasy football league's group text. It reads your league's public Sleeper data and the messages in that chat, and sends messages such as weekly recaps, injury alerts, and trade summaries.

Opt-in. A league commissioner adds the Commish number to a group chat. Presence in that group chat constitutes consent to receive messages in it.

Opt-out. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help and contact information. You may also leave the group chat.
