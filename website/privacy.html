Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-08-16

The short version. Commish reads your league's group chat so it can respond when addressed, and reads your Sleeper league so it can write about it. It stores phone numbers to tell managers apart. It does not sell your phone number, and it does not share your phone number or messages with third parties for their own marketing. Message content is sent to an AI provider to generate replies. Details below.

Who we are

Commish is operated by {{BUSINESS_NAME}}, based in {{JURISDICTION_SHORT}}, United States. Reach us any time at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. That address is monitored and is the fastest way to reach a person. If you need a postal address for a formal request, ask and we will provide one.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your mobile phone number To send messages to your league's group chat, and to tell which manager is which so the agent can address you correctly.
Messages in the group chat the agent is part of To decide whether it has been addressed, and to answer questions with context. Messages are stored so the agent can follow a conversation rather than reacting to each text in isolation.
Your Sleeper league data Rosters, lineups, scores, transactions. This is what recaps, injury alerts, and trade grades are built from. Retrieved from Sleeper's public API.
Your display name and team name So messages refer to you the way your league already does.

We do not currently take payment at all. Commish is free for its founding leagues through the 2026 season, so there is no card on file and no payment processor involved. If that changes we will say so here first, and card details would be handled by a payment provider rather than stored by us. We also never ask for your Sleeper password; Commish only reads data Sleeper already makes public.

Who your data goes to

We use these providers to run the service. They process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Sendblue: delivers and receives the text messages.
  • Supabase: hosts the database where league data, messages, and phone numbers are stored.
  • Render: runs the server software.
  • Anthropic: generates the recap text and the agent's replies. Recent group chat messages and your league's statistics are sent to Anthropic's API for this. Your phone number is not included in what is sent for text generation.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your mobile phone number or the contents of your messages with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.
  • We do not use your league's data to advertise to you.
  • We do not message you individually unless you have messaged us first or your commissioner has set you up to receive approval requests.

How long we keep it

League data and messages are kept while your league is active, because the agent's value depends on remembering past seasons. If you cancel or ask for deletion, we remove your league's data within 30 days, except where we must keep records for legal or accounting reasons.

Your choices

  • Stop the messages. Reply STOP in the chat, leave the group, or ask your commissioner to remove the number.
  • Get a copy of your data, or have it deleted. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We will verify you control the phone number before acting on the request.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data. We honor these regardless of where you live.

A group chat is a shared space

Anything you write in a group chat is visible to everyone else in it, including the agent. The agent may quote or summarize league statistics in messages it sends to that same group. It does not repeat your messages to anyone outside the group.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting providers. Access to production data is limited to people who need it to operate the service. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Commish is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has been added to a league, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will remove the data.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date above and notify active leagues in their group chat before the change takes effect.
