Messaging terms

Last updated 2026-08-16

What you are agreeing to when Commish is part of your league's group text.

How you opt in

Your league commissioner adds the Commish number to your league's group text. Being in that group chat is the opt-in, so messages arrive in the thread you're already part of. Nobody is messaged individually without asking for it first.

How you opt out

Reply STOP at any time and you'll get no further messages. Reply HELP for contact information. You can also simply leave the group chat, or ask your commissioner to remove the number from it.

Typically a handful of messages a week in season. Far fewer outside it, where there is little beyond the occasional trade. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

What we do with your number

Your phone number is used to send these messages and to tell managers apart within your league. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing. Details are in the privacy policy.
