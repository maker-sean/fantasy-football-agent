Before we start

The agreement.

You're adding an automated agent to a group chat with other people in it. By continuing you confirm you have permission to add a number to that chat, and that you'll tell your league it's there.

Step 1 of 4

Which league?

Your Sleeper username — we'll pull up your leagues.

Step 2 of 4

Who's who.

Put a name and mobile number against each team. This is how the agent knows who it's talking to — and who to blame.

These numbers are only used to recognise people inside your group chat, and they are never shared or sold. The agent posts in the group — nobody gets a direct text unless you send them one. Leave any you don't have; the agent asks for them in the chat.

Step 3 of 4

What should it answer to?

It stays quiet in your group chat unless someone says its name. Pick whatever your league will actually type.

This sets the recaps and the replies.

Step 4 of 4

Add it to your group chat.

Last step. Add this number to your league's group text, then send any message in that chat — a "hey" will do. It only needs to hear from the chat once to know it is in the right place.
